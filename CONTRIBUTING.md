# Contributing to Tunnel R.A.T.

Thanks for your interest! This is a community fork — issues and pull requests are welcome.

- **License:** by contributing you agree your changes are released under **GPL-3.0**
  (the project's license). Keep upstream attribution intact (see [`CREDITS.md`](CREDITS.md)).
- **Build & test:** `./fbt updater_package`, then flash the resulting `.tgz` with
  qFlipper or the Flipper app (*Install from file*). CI also builds on every push.
- **Scope:** this fork tracks Momentum. Please don't forward fork-specific changes to
  upstream Momentum — their project has its own contribution policy.
- Be respectful, and describe what your change does and why.
